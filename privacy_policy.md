# Privacy Policy for MessengerUP

**Last Updated:** January 2025

Welcome to **MessengerUP**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our application and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

## 1. Important Information and Who We Are
MessengerUP is a modified client of the Telegram Messenger application, designed to provide additional features such as UP Premium and UP Stars.

## 2. The Data We Collect About You
We do not collect any personal data beyond what the official Telegram Messenger API collects. Your data is handled entirely by the official Telegram servers.
- **Identity Data:** includes username, first name, last name, and profile pictures.
- **Contact Data:** includes phone number.
- **Usage Data:** handled by Telegram's core infrastructure.

## 3. How We Use Your Personal Data
We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. Most commonly, we will use your personal data in the following circumstances:
- To provide our core messaging functionality.
- To process in-app rewards (e.g., UP Premium features and UP Stars).

## 4. Disclosures of Your Personal Data
Your data is stored and processed by the official Telegram Messenger servers. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

## 5. Data Security
Because we use the official Telegram API, your messages are secured by MTProto encryption. We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way.

## 6. Your Legal Rights
You have the right to:
- Request access to your personal data.
- Request correction of your personal data.
- Request erasure of your personal data (Account Deletion).

**Contact us:** If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact our support bot within the app: @messengeruppremium_bot.
